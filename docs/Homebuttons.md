# Hero Home Buttons & Database Alignment Specification

## Overview
This document defines the production Hero Home Buttons for RealtyPals, aligned 100% with the master dataset in `newProj/75`. 
These hero buttons guide first-time and returning users by demonstrating our platform's capabilities across our primary coverage sectors.

All 7 hero button groups are fully backed by project database records, unit configurations, pricing, statuses (ready to move vs. under construction), builder profiles, and connectivity metadata in the database.

---

## 1. Sector 75 Noida (`sec75`)
- **Database Scope**: 20 Projects (Maxblis Grand Kingston, Gardenia Golf City, AIMS Golf Avenue, Panchsheel Pratishtha, DASNAC Burj Noida, etc.)
- **Available Typologies**: 2 BHK, 2.5 BHK, 3 BHK, 4 BHK, Penthouses (Maxblis White House)
- **Status Breakdown**: 15 Ready to Move, 5 Under Construction
- **Key Features**: Sector 50 & 76 Aqua Line Metro Station proximity (<700m - 1.2km)
